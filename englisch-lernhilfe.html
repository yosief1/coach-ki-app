Reading comprehension

Lies den Text und beantworte anschließend die Fragen.

A greener school

Last year, students at Greenhill School noticed that their school produced a lot of rubbish. Mia and her friend Leo started a recycling club. At first, only six students joined. They put recycling boxes in every classroom and made posters explaining what could be recycled.

After a few months, more students became interested. The club has also planted flowers in the school garden. Mia says that small actions can make a big difference. Next term, the students are going to ask the headteacher for a place to grow vegetables.

Listening comprehension

Höre dir den Dialog an. Du kannst ihn mehrmals abspielen. Beantworte die Fragen, bevor du den Text einblendest.

At the school café

Alex: Have you tried the new school café, Sam?
Sam: Yes, I went there yesterday. They sell sandwiches and fruit.
Alex: Have they got any vegetarian food?
Sam: Yes. I had a cheese sandwich. It was really good.
Alex: Great! I have been bringing lunch from home all week, but tomorrow I’ll try the café.

Writing a comment

Topic: “Schools should have a longer lunch break.” What do you think?

Schreibe einen kurzen Comment auf Englisch. Begründe deine Meinung und nenne mindestens ein Beispiel.

  • Beginne mit deiner Meinung: In my opinion… / I believe…
  • Nenne mindestens zwei Gründe: Firstly… / Also…
  • Gib ein Beispiel: For example…
  • Schließe mit einem Fazit: To sum up…
  • Prüfe Rechtschreibung, Zeitformen und Satzzeichen.

0 Wörter

Tipp: Ein guter Comment hat eine klare Meinung, passende Gründe und eine verständliche Struktur.
